# PROBE: every level-select carousel entry's stock art is preloaded, none decodes cold.
#
# PINS: ScreenshotSaver.StockShots, which since card 8d6883f3 is DERIVED -- every level with
# LevelArt.HasCarouselEntry contributes its LevelArt.ScreenshotPath, deduped. Both consumers
# (ScreenshotSaver.Init and Game1.QueueMenuWarm) iterate that one list, and SubMenuLevelChoice
# resolves each carousel entry's image through the same ScreenshotPath lookup.
#
# FAILURE MEANS: a carousel entry's art is no longer in the preloaded set, so it decodes
# (900x700, ~30-40ms in Chrome) the first time the player opens that carousel. The quoted line
# names the asset. That is EXACTLY the bug card 4d47c5ba fixed: ScreenshotSaver.Init hardcoded
# eleven of the twelve and the one it missed -- gfx/screenshots/webcamss, the challenge
# carousel's LAST entry -- decoded cold on first opening Challenges, unnoticed for however long
# it had been true. Fix by restoring the level to LevelArt.HasCarouselEntry / ScreenshotPath,
# NOT by re-hardcoding a list.
#
# WHY IT OPENS BOTH CAROUSELS: `SubMenuLevelChoice.loadScreenshots` loads EVERY entry's image
# in one loop, so merely opening a carousel demands all of its art -- no walking required. But
# there are TWO of them, `levelSelector` (the three missions) and `challengeSelector` (the nine
# challenges), each with its own Initialize. Opening only Challenges leaves level1/2/3empty
# undemanded, and a probe that did that PASSED with Levels.Level1 deleted from
# HasCarouselEntry (measured). So: Start Game to open the mission carousel, back out, then
# Challenges.
#
# THE POSITIVE CONTROL is the launch at the end. `expect-not COLD ... gfx/screenshots/` passes
# beautifully on a run that never opened a carousel at all -- and it very nearly shipped that
# way here, because ?autostart silently swallows menu keypresses and the whole run sat on the
# main menu looking green. `[loadprofile] TeamChallenge preload:` can only be reached by
# navigating into the CHALLENGE carousel and walking seven entries along it, so it proves the
# window this probe asserts over is the real one. Boot with ?menu (what the other menu-driven
# probe uses), never ?autostart. The mission leg has no equivalent control of its own: it is
# covered transitively, since the same keypress stream has to have worked to reach the launch.
#
# WHERE THE `mark` GOES, and this is the whole probe: a SubMenuLevelChoice loads its entry art
# in its own Initialize, which KNI runs when the submenu is first ADDED -- i.e. the moment the
# player opens that carousel, not when the MenuScene is built. So a dropped level's art decodes
# in the beat between pressing select and the carousel appearing. Mark AFTER the settle but
# BEFORE navigating: a mark placed after the carousel is up sits one step too late, the
# offending decode lands in the dropped window, and the probe passes on the very mutation it
# exists to catch (measured -- it did).
#
# Under ?menu the splash is skipped and the warm pump never runs, so all twelve decode inside
# ScreenshotSaver.Init during boot. That is the debug path, not a regression, and is what the
# mark drops -- leaving the carousel's own late fetch, which is the signal.
#
# ?unlockall so a wiped save can select the later challenges; ?invuln so the launched level
# does not die during the settle.
#
# eahl: --flags "?menu&noattract&loadlog&unlockall&invuln"

step 150 nodraw

# Boot decodes the whole stock set (ScreenshotSaver.Init) under the (boot) sentinel; that is
# the set being defended, and dropping it here is what leaves only decodes the carousel had to
# go and FETCH. The mark must sit BEFORE the navigation below -- see the header.
mark

# Start Game -> the MISSION carousel (its Initialize decodes level1/2/3empty), then back out.
eval Press enter 1
step 90 nodraw
eval Press esc 1
step 90 nodraw

# Main menu -> Challenges (three down from Start), then into the carousel. Its Initialize
# decodes all nine challenge thumbnails.
eval Press down 1
step 90 nodraw
eval Press down 1
step 90 nodraw
eval Press down 1
step 90 nodraw
eval Press enter 1
step 90 nodraw

# Walk to Team Challenge (index 7 of 9) and launch it -- the positive control. The walk buys no
# extra COLD coverage (the carousel already loaded every entry above); it is only how the run
# reaches a launchable entry, and how the control proves the navigation worked.
eval Press right 1
step 30 nodraw
eval Press right 1
step 30 nodraw
eval Press right 1
step 30 nodraw
eval Press right 1
step 30 nodraw
eval Press right 1
step 30 nodraw
eval Press right 1
step 30 nodraw
eval Press right 1
step 60 nodraw

# Difficulty screen, then select.
eval Press enter 1
step 120 nodraw
eval Press enter 1
step 240 nodraw

expect \[loadprofile\] TeamChallenge preload:
expect-not COLD decode in .*: gfx/screenshots/
quit
