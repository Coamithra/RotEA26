### AI: DoAIFire does not LEAD its target -- it shoots where the enemy was

Card `5c89e4e6`, filed 2026-08-08.

**Owner verdict (2026-08-09): rejected.** Aim spread exists precisely so the bot does not shoot perfectly -- fallibility is the design (a per-tier skill knob). A proper lead would also need each enemy's real motion model, which lives per-type, not in a generic class; ObservedVelocity is not a reliable substitute (scripted movers, pool-recycle teleports). Not worth doing.

Found while doing card bb949dd9 (boss standoff / gun reach credits the hull radius).

PlayerShip.DoAIFire aims with FireAt(VectorToAngle(nearest.Position - Position) + aimSpread) -- straight at the target's CURRENT position, no lead, on every target. The 2008 original did the same (src_decompiled/EvilAliens/PlayerShip.cs ~line 549), so this is inherited, not a port regression.

Why it matters MORE now: card bb949dd9 moved the boss standoff outward by the boss's hull radius (MarsBoss r* 175 -> ~300px edge, BrainBoss 118 -> ~276px), so the bullet's flight time to the target roughly doubled. Bullets travel 0.78 px/ms, so a shot at the new max reach is ~450ms in flight; anything moving crosswise at even 0.2 px/ms is ~90px from where it was aimed.

The bot has everything it needs: AlienDrawableGameComponent.ObservedVelocity is already measured from real position deltas and is already what DoAIMove's cone/evade terms use. A first-order lead (solve for the intercept along the bullet's constant 0.78 px/ms, fall back to the current position if no solution) is the obvious shape.

Measure it, do not assume it: rigs are marsboss (?level=Level2&marsboss) and brainboss (?level=Level3&brainboss) via python tools/sim/ai_sweep.py, N=60 paired by seed per web CLAUDE.md's measurement bar. The metric is TIME-TO-VICTORY (a lead that works kills faster), with deaths and killers= as the regression columns. Note aim spread (PI/12 at Very_Hard) may swamp the lead on small targets -- a null result is a real possible outcome and worth recording either way.
