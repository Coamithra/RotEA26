Drag the parked ship, its shadow, and the flying-sprite ghost into place, then export Content/data/landed_offsets.json. Design space is 800×600, the same as the game.

Drag with the mouse, or click an element then nudge with arrow keys (Shift = 10px). Wheel over the shadow to resize it. The anchor ✛ is where the game places the ship (its Position) — offsets are measured from it.
